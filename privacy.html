Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

ClipFarm ("the app") is a desktop tool that runs entirely on the user's own computer. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is handled.

Data the app accesses

When you connect a YouTube channel, you sign in with Google and grant the app permission to:

  • Upload videos to your YouTube channel (youtube.upload);
  • Read basic channel information, such as the channel name and ID (youtube.readonly), to show which channel is connected.

How data is used and stored

  • OAuth tokens and channel names are stored only in a local database on your computer.
  • The app does not send your data to any server operated by the developer. It has no such server.
  • Data is used only to upload the videos you scheduled to the channels you connected.
  • Data is not sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

You can disconnect a channel in the app at any time, and revoke the app's access in your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's data folder removes all stored tokens.
